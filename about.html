About us

Practical AI education, built from Tamil Nadu.

Our story

Enjoy Creator Academy was founded by B. Sriram Marudhaiyappan, whose journey into artificial intelligence began at just 16 years old. After stepping away from traditional education, he went deep into AI and technology — learning entirely by building, testing and creating, rather than through a conventional classroom.

At 17, that hands-on experience became Enjoy Creator Academy: a practical, no-fluff AI education academy built for people who want to actually create, grow and build new opportunities with artificial intelligence — not just talk about it.

Our mission

We believe AI education should be accessible, practical, and taught in a way that makes sense — regardless of your background or prior technical experience. Every class we run is built around real output: a real prompt, a real video, a real automation, a real skill you keep long after the class ends.

What we've built so far

  • 200+ creators trained through our live classes in the first six months of 2026.
  • A structured curriculum covering AI fundamentals, prompt engineering, AI video creation, full-stack AI development, and business automation.
  • A growing community of students who use these skills in their own content, businesses and careers.

Who we teach

Our students range from complete beginners with no technical background to working creators and business owners looking to add AI to their existing skill set. Every class is designed to meet you where you are.
