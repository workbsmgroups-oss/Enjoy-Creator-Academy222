Frequently asked questions

Before you register, read this.

Do I need any prior experience with AI or design to join?
No. The class is built for complete beginners. We start from what AI actually is and build up to full video creation, step by step.
What do I need to attend the live class?
A phone or laptop, a stable internet connection, and a Google account. All tools used in class will be shared with you before we start.
Will I get a certificate after the class?
Yes. Every student who completes the 7-day live class receives an official Enjoy Creator Academy certificate of completion.
Is the class in Tamil or English?
The class is taught in Tamil, with English used for technical terms and on-screen tool instructions, so it's easy to follow either way.
What if I miss a live session?
We recommend attending live for the best experience. Reach out to us on WhatsApp if you miss a session, and we'll guide you on catching up.
Can I get a refund after registering?
Registrations are non-refundable once confirmed, since seats are limited and reserved specifically for you. Please see our Refund Policy for full details.
Do I need to buy any paid AI tools?
No. We teach using free and low-cost AI tools first, and show you how to scale up only if and when you choose to.
Is this class only for YouTubers and content creators?
Not at all. Business owners, students, marketers and freelancers all use these same AI video and automation skills in their own work.
How is this different from free YouTube tutorials?
Live, structured teaching with direct doubt-clearing, a set 7-day roadmap, and a mentor who has actually built a business on these exact skills — not scattered videos.
How do I get support after I register?
You'll get access to our WhatsApp support line, where our team answers questions before, during and after the live class.
