Frequently asked questions

Before you register, read this.

Do I need any prior experience with AI or design to join?
No. The course is built for complete beginners. We start from the basics and build up to practical AI video creation step by step.
What do I need to attend the course?
A phone or laptop, a stable internet connection, and a Google account. All tools used in the course will be shared with you.
Will I get a certificate after the course?
Yes. Students who complete the AI Video Creation Course receive an official Enjoy Creator Academy certificate of completion.
Is the course in Tamil or English?
The course is taught in Tamil, with English used for technical terms and on-screen tool instructions, so it's easy to follow either way.
Do I need to buy any paid AI tools?
No. We teach using free and low-cost AI tools first, and show you how to scale up only if and when you choose to.
Is this course only for YouTubers and content creators?
Not at all. Business owners, students, marketers and freelancers can use the same AI video creation skills in their own work.
How is this different from free YouTube tutorials?
The course provides a structured learning path, practical workflows and direct guidance instead of scattered tutorials.
Can I get a refund after registering?
Registrations are non-refundable once confirmed, since seats are limited and reserved specifically for you. Please see our Refund Policy for full details.
How do I get support after I register?
You'll get access to our WhatsApp support line, where our team answers questions before, during and after the course.
