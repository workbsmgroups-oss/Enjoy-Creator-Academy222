Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we collect

When you register for our live classes or contact us, we may collect your name, phone number (including WhatsApp number), email address, and any details you choose to share with us through our registration form or messages.

2. How we use your information

  • To confirm your registration for a live class and send you class links, schedules and reminders.
  • To respond to your questions on WhatsApp, email or Instagram.
  • To share updates about future batches, offers or academy announcements, where you have not opted out.
  • To improve our classes and website based on feedback.

3. How we protect your information

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. Registration data is collected through Google Forms and stored securely by Google under their own security practices.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your information may be shared with trusted service providers (such as Google Forms, WhatsApp and email providers) strictly to operate our classes and communicate with you.

Our website may use basic, essential cookies or local browser storage to remember your preferences and improve performance. We do not use invasive tracking or sell browsing data to advertisers.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to delete the personal information we hold about you, at any time by writing to us at work.enjoycreatoracademy@gmail.com.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "last updated" date shown above.
