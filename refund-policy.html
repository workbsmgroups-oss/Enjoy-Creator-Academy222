Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. No refund policy

All payments made to Enjoy Creator Academy for live classes, master classes or any other paid program are strictly non-refundable. This applies regardless of attendance, satisfaction with the class, or reason for the request, once a registration is confirmed.

2. Why we don't offer refunds

Our live classes have limited seats that are reserved specifically for registered students at the time of payment. Offering refunds after registration would take away a confirmed seat from another student who could have joined in your place.

3. Rescheduled or cancelled classes

In the rare event that Enjoy Creator Academy cancels or reschedules a live class from our side, registered students will be offered a seat in the next available batch at no extra cost. This is the only circumstance under which an alternative to a refund is provided.

4. Before you register

Please read the class details, curriculum, schedule and pricing carefully on our website before registering. If you have any questions, contact us on WhatsApp or email before you complete your payment — our team is happy to help you decide if a class is right for you.

5. Payment disputes

Raising a payment dispute or chargeback with your bank or payment provider instead of contacting us directly may result in your access to the class and any related community being suspended while the matter is resolved.

6. Contact us

For any questions about this policy, reach us before registering at work.enjoycreatoracademy@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +91 63810 46840.
